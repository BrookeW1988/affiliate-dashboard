# Paying affiliates

The dashboard tracks what you owe. You pay outside it (usually a bank transfer), then record what you paid so the numbers stay right.

## The numbers on your dashboard

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| Revenue | What buyers paid through their link, minus refunds |
| Earned | Their commission on that revenue, all time |
| Paid | What you've recorded paying them, all time |
| Owed | Earned minus Paid: what you owe them right now |

Each sale keeps the rate the affiliate had when it happened. Changing someone's rate never changes what they've already earned.

## When to pay

Pick a rhythm and put it in `payoutTerms` so affiliates know: monthly, after a launch, after an event. Wait until your refund window has passed for the sales you're paying on. That way a refund doesn't take back commission you've already sent.

## Recording a payout

1. Pay them (bank transfer, PayPal, however you've agreed).
2. On your dashboard, click **Record payout** next to their name. It suggests what's owed. Change it if you paid a different amount.
3. Add a note for your records if you like ("bank transfer, invoice 12").

Owed drops by that amount, and their stats page shows it under "paid to you".

## Refunds after you've paid

If a sale is refunded after you've paid commission on it, their Earned goes down and Owed can dip below zero. That's a credit: take it off their next payout.

## For your bookkeeper

**Download as a spreadsheet** (under the leaderboard) gives everyone's numbers, including totals paid and the last payout date. It doesn't include individual payouts: the dates and amounts you sent are in your bank account too.

If your affiliates are businesses, they may send you an invoice for their commission. How commission is treated for tax (and whether GST applies) depends on your situation and theirs, so check with your accountant.
